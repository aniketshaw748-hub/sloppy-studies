[dramatic] What if one machine run cost a fortune, but stocking the parts cost one too? [pause] In EPQ, the Economic Production Quantity, the two most important costs are setup cost and holding cost. Setup cost is paid every production run, so bigger batches make it fall. Holding cost is paid on stored stock, so bigger batches make it rise. Total cost is lowest where the two curves cross. Memory trick: setup falls, holding climbs, meet in the middle. Write that. Five marks. Secured.
