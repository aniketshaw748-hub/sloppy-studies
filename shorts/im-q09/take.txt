[pause] A BOM, Bill of Materials, is the complete list of raw materials, components and sub-assemblies, with quantities, needed to make one unit of a product. It is drawn as a product structure tree. Level zero is the end item, level one the sub-assemblies, level two the parts. It is the key input to MRP, which plans what to buy. Memory trick: BOM is the recipe card. Write that. Five marks.
